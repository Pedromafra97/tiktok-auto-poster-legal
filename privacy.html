Privacy Policy — TikTok Auto Poster

Last updated: July 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how TikTok Auto Poster ("the App") handles information. The App is a personal automation tool used solely by its developer.

2. Information We Access

The App only accesses the TikTok account explicitly connected and authorized by its developer through TikTok's OAuth 2.0 login flow. This includes the minimum permissions required to publish video content (video.publish scope). No data belonging to any other TikTok user is accessed, collected, or stored.

3. Content Generation

Video scripts, narration audio, and rendered video files are generated using third-party AI and media services (such as language models, text-to-speech engines, and video rendering APIs) based on inputs provided by the developer. This generated content is published directly to the developer's own connected TikTok account.

4. Data Storage

The App does not maintain a database of user information. Any credentials (such as OAuth tokens) are stored securely and used only to authorize publishing to the developer's own account.

5. Data Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, other than the service providers necessary to generate and publish content (e.g., AI and video rendering services), which process data solely to fulfill their function within the workflow.

6. Data Retention

OAuth tokens and related credentials are retained only as long as necessary to operate the App and may be revoked at any time by the developer.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
